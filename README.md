# Customer Behavior Analysis

This project analyzes customer behavior, revenue drivers, and discount effectiveness using Python, SQL, and Power BI. The goal is to uncover actionable insights that support business decision-making and product strategy.

Link Notion for Project Management: https://www.notion.so/Customer-Behavior-Analysis-Dashboard-34e6c310200d8068af68db390b7adff1?source=copy_link

---


## Project Overview

This project focuses on understanding:
- Customer segments and purchasing behavior
- Key revenue drivers across categories and locations
- The effectiveness of discount strategies

The analysis combines:
- **Python** for data cleaning and exploratory analysis (EDA)
- **SQL** for querying and aggregating business insights
- **Power BI** for building interactive dashboards

---

## Key Insights

- Revenue is primarily driven by a strong base of loyal customers.
- Customer spending (AOV) is stable across segments, indicating growth depends on purchase frequency.
- Revenue is highly concentrated in key categories (e.g., Clothing).
- Discount strategies show limited effectiveness and may reduce order value.
- Customer behavior follows structured purchase cycles (e.g., 14-day, 90-day).

---

## Dashboards

The Power BI dashboard is divided into 4 main sections:

1. **Overview**
   - Business performance KPIs
   - Revenue and customer distribution

2. **Customer Behavior**
   - Customer segmentation
   - Purchase frequency patterns

3. **Revenue Analysis**
   - Revenue by category, product, and location
   - Contribution analysis

4. **Discount Impact**
   - Discount vs non-discount performance
   - Impact on AOV and customer behavior
---

## Limitations

- The dataset is skewed toward repeat customers, which may overestimate retention performance.
- Some anomalies exist (e.g., no female customers using discounts), which may indicate data quality issues.
- Limited variation across segments (AOV, geography, seasonality) reduces analytical depth.
- Lack of time-based data prevents analysis of customer lifecycle and long-term trends.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Shift from broad discounting to targeted, behavior-based incentives
- Focus on increasing purchase frequency rather than order value
- Invest in high-performing product categories
- Leverage loyal customers through engagement and cross-selling strategies
